# Spaces

A space is a workspace with its own bots, chats, memory and computer. You get one when you sign up,
and spaces other people share with you appear next to it. You switch between them from the sidebar.

## What stays inside a space

Nothing crosses between spaces unless you allow it:

- Chats, files and [memory](./memory.md).
- The Team Computer and every bot's [computer](./computer.md).
- [App connections](./integrations.md).
- [Saved credentials](./bot-secrets.md), when the space keeps its own.
- Skills a bot saves.

## What follows you everywhere

Your account settings apply in every space: [models](./models.md), [voice](./voice-and-calls.md),
[approvals](./approvals.md) and your credentials vault. Skills, MCP servers and plugins you add are
available in all your spaces too.

## Share a space

Open the workspace menu and choose **Invite people** to copy a link. Anyone with it can join until it
expires after 7 days. Whoever opens it joins that workspace only; it shows up in their workspace list
next to their own, and your other workspaces stay private.

Everyone in a shared space sees and can change its bots, chats, memory and routines, and uses one
Team Computer and browser, so a site someone signs in to there stays signed in for everyone.

Each person keeps their own models, connected accounts, MCP servers, skills, vault and approval
rules. A bot uses the accounts of whoever messaged it, a routine uses its owner's, and each person's
runs are billed to their own account. Only the person a bot is waiting on can take over its
computer for them.

The owner can remove people. Anyone else can **Leave**. What a person who leaves made in the space,
such as bots and routines, passes to the owner, with routines paused, and the app accounts they
connected there are disconnected. Their skills, MCP servers and plugins stay theirs in their other
spaces.

## Space settings

Right-click a space in the sidebar and choose **Settings**, or open **Settings → General** and choose
**Manage** next to the space. There you can rename the space and manage its members.
